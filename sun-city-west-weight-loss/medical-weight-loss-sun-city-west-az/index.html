    How Medical Weight Loss Works in Sun City West

    Medical weight loss treats excess weight as a chronic condition. A licensed provider reviews your history, labs and medications to find what is driving your weight, such as insulin resistance, poor sleep or a medication that raises appetite. Prescriptions are optional. GLP-1 medicines like semaglutide and tirzepatide are common tools because they quiet appetite and help you feel full sooner.

    Who Medical Weight Loss Is For

    Adults who have not reached a healthy weight with diet and exercise alone are the usual fit. Prescription medicines are generally considered for a BMI of 30 or more, or 27 or more with a weight-related condition like high blood pressure, type 2 diabetes, high cholesterol or sleep apnea. Lower BMIs can still get guidance. Pregnancy rules out medical weight loss medication.

    Treatment Schedule in Sun City West

    Week one is the intake and review. A weekly injection plan steps up every four weeks, while a daily pill plan usually means one tablet each morning, sometimes building over a month. Check-ins come every few weeks early on. Once you are stable, check-ins usually space out to every one to three months.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    The first two weeks focus on habits and, if prescribed, a starting dose. GLP-1 patients often feel less hungry by week two, and nausea or constipation tends to cluster right after the first dose and each step up. Expect slow, steady change. Your provider will set a realistic weekly target, often around 1 to 2 pounds.

    Side Effects in Sun City West

    Side effects depend on the treatment. GLP-1 medicines commonly cause nausea, diarrhea, constipation and reduced appetite, while oral appetite medicines can cause dry mouth, trouble sleeping or a faster heart rate. Fast loss raises gallstone risk. Protein at every meal and resistance exercise two or three times a week help protect muscle while you lose weight.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Screening covers your BMI, conditions, medications and weight history. Certain medicines are ruled out by a personal or family history of medullary thyroid cancer or MEN 2, uncontrolled high blood pressure, pregnancy or a history of eating disorders. Recent bloodwork helps. Common asks are A1C, a lipid panel, thyroid tests and a metabolic panel from the past year.

    Medical Weight Loss Compared With Other Approaches in Sun City West

    Commercial diet programs cannot prescribe. They focus on food and behavior, while medical weight loss adds a licensed provider who can treat appetite hormones, manage related conditions and adjust medications that may be adding weight. Treatment options include FDA-approved injections, oral medicines and compounded versions that lack FDA approval. Surgery remains an option for some.
