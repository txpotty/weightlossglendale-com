    How NAD+ Injection Therapy Works in Phoenix, AZ

    How NAD+ Works in Phoenix

    NAD+ is nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide. Cells use it to move electrons while making ATP, and enzymes like sirtuins and PARPs burn through it while repairing DNA and handling stress. Older tissue tends to have less. Injected under the skin, NAD+ skips digestion entirely, but researchers are still working out how much of it reaches muscle, brain and other tissue.

    Who NAD+ Injections Are Usually For

    Adults interested in energy, mental clarity, exercise recovery and healthy aging are the usual candidates. People on semaglutide or tirzepatide sometimes add it. It is not a weight loss medication and does not treat any specific disease, and people who are pregnant, breastfeeding, have active cancer or significant liver or kidney disease are generally not candidates.

    NAD+ Dosing and Schedule in Phoenix

    Small shots, slow push. A common start is roughly 50 milligrams under the skin a few times weekly, with increases every week or two depending on how you feel. Use the belly or thigh with a fine needle. Pushing the plunger over about a minute cuts down on flushing.

    What to Expect in the First Month

    Week one is about technique. Many people feel warmth, flushing, mild nausea or a tight feeling right after injecting, and it usually fades within minutes. Over weeks two to four, some patients report steadier energy or clearer thinking, while others notice little change. Doses change based on your notes.

    NAD+ Injection Side Effects in Phoenix

    Flushing leads the list. Other frequent ones are stinging at the site, nausea, headache and feeling lightheaded. Chest tightness, muscle cramps or a racing heart can happen if the dose is too high or pushed too fast, and these usually improve with a lower dose or slower injection. Hives or trouble breathing mean call 911.

    Eligibility and Screening

    The review starts with history and your supplement list. You will be asked about pregnancy, cancer history, liver and kidney health, heart conditions and any NMN, nicotinamide riboside or niacin you already take. Labs are not always required. Upload recent labs if you have them.

    NAD+ Injections Compared to Other Options in Phoenix

    IV infusions are the usual comparison. An NAD+ drip delivers a larger amount in one sitting but can take two to four hours, and many people feel more nausea and cramping during the infusion. NAD+ nasal spray avoids needles at a smaller dose. NMN and NR capsules work differently, as building blocks.
