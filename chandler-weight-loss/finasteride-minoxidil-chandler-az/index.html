    How Finasteride + Minoxidil Works in Chandler, AZ

    How Finasteride + Minoxidil Works in Chandler

    Finasteride blocks type 2 5-alpha reductase, the enzyme that turns testosterone into DHT. With less DHT around, the follicles on the crown and temples stop shrinking as quickly, and many hold their size for years instead of fading out. Minoxidil adds something different. It keeps hairs in their growing phase longer, so they come in thicker.

    Who This Combination Is For

    It is mainly for adult men with male pattern hair loss, such as a receding hairline or thinning crown. It works best when started early, while follicles are still producing hair, because areas that have been smooth for years rarely come back with any medicine. Postmenopausal women sometimes use finasteride off-label. Women who could become pregnant cannot.

    Dosing and Daily Schedule in Chandler

    Oral finasteride is FDA-approved at 1 mg once a day for male pattern hair loss. Minoxidil comes as a twice-daily topical or a low-dose off-label tablet. One spray daily also works. A compounded topical that combines finasteride and minoxidil goes on the scalp once a day.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Expect some shedding in months one and two. That early shed usually comes from minoxidil pushing resting hairs out so new growing hairs can take their place, and it typically settles by month three. Around month six, density starts to improve. Long-term trials showed most men held their hair for years. Full results show near month twelve.

    Side Effects of Finasteride + Minoxidil in Chandler

    Finasteride may lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in about one to two percent of men. These usually resolve after stopping, and rare mood changes, including low mood, should be reported to your provider right away. Topical minoxidil can itch or flake. The oral form sometimes causes fluid retention or a quicker pulse. Topical finasteride may lower whole-body exposure.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, heart conditions, blood pressure, mood history and sexual health. They also review every medication and supplement, and they ask whether you plan to have children soon, since finasteride can slightly lower sperm count in some men. PSA results drop by about half. Pregnant partners should not touch broken tablets.

    How It Compares to Other Options in Chandler

    Finasteride + minoxidil is the standard first combination for men. Dutasteride + minoxidil is a stronger, off-label option, usually considered after a year on finasteride without enough change in the photos. Minoxidil + GHK-Cu is topical-only. Minoxidil by itself leaves the hormonal cause untreated.
